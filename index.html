Before you start

LeanLog stores what you enter so it can show your workouts, progress and charts:

  • Your name and email address from your account.
  • Your workouts, exercises and settings.
  • Body measurements you log in Metrics, such as your weight. Under the GDPR these count as health data.

Your data is used only to run the app for you. It isn't shared or sold, and it isn't used for advertising. It's stored in Google Firebase (Belgium). Sign-in runs on Amazon Cognito (Sweden) and the app's server on Microsoft Azure (Ireland).

You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account, which deletes all your data.
